## Disenchantry

Disenchantry 1.3.0 by x_Raven_x, https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/disenchantry. Based on Disenchant Helper by
Rakuga, https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/disenchant-helper.

Taken from it:
- the rough disenchant estimate for the rare item-level brackets WoW Forever added (item level 5-15 and 16-20), in
  `tools/disenchant_data.py` and the generated `Disenchant.lua` (source "d");
- the idea of the bag list with a material forecast and a one-click Disenchant button, in `DisenchantPreview.lua`
  (our own code).
